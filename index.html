How to Start SSC Preparation

Phase 1: Understand the Battlefield (Week 1)

Analyze the syllabus thoroughly. Attempt one previous year question (PYQ) paper blindly to gauge your current standing. Decide if you fall into the "crowd" or the "competition".

Phase 2: Concept Building (Months 1-3)

Focus on clearing basic concepts for Maths, Reasoning, English, and GS. Do not rush for mock tests yet. Take topic-wise quizzes.

Phase 3: Rigorous Practice & PYQs (Months 4-5)

Solve last 3-4 years of TCS pattern PYQs. Start giving sectional mocks. Build typing speed concurrently (15 mins a day).

Phase 4: Full Mocks & Revision (Month 6)

Attempt 1 full mock daily. Spend equal time analyzing mistakes. Revise GK notes and English vocab daily.
